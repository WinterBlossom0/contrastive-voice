            Analyse your own recording

            An uploaded recording is compared as a different performance (regime R2). Its analysis states the reference mode (one reading, or the median of five readings of a bundled bank) and reports which dimensions are scored, listed or displayed. Limits: .

              Deviations are relative to the reference: one rendition, or the word-by-word median of five readings of a bundled bank. Read the analysis notes for timing resolution and scoring limits. The flaws in the examples are controlled acoustic edits, and no human ratings back the scores.
