# Listening test of "fix your speech": pre-registered design and analysis

Written 2026-10-10 (IST), before any listener data. Kit version: `kit_version` in `key.json` (a877f972 at writing);
every listener CSV carries the version, and `score.py` refuses rows of another version. Any change to this file after
the first listener CSV arrives goes under "Changes after data" at the end, with the reason.

## 1. Question

Does the shipped repair (`repair.fix_finding`, `pitch_method="own"`, the product default) make the delivery of a flagged
spot better for human listeners, and what does it cost in naturalness? The detector's "no longer flagged" result cannot
answer this (stage review: academic findings 1 and 10; the target and the judge are the same detector).

## 2. Design (MUSHRA-style, after ITU-R BS.1534-3, adapted)

Per item: one spot (the words of one finding) with 1 s of context on each side, in four versions, in a random order
labelled A to D:

| condition | what it is |
|---|---|
| reference (hidden) | the clean source recording before the injected edit, cut at the same words; its context is sample-identical to the flawed clip (normalised correlation 1.000 at both ends for every item, `key.json` `reference_match`) |
| original | the flawed clip as the product receives it |
| fixed | the flawed clip after `repair.fix_finding(..., pitch_method="own")`, cut at the mapped words (`repair.listen_pairs`) |
| anchor (low) | the original low-passed at 3.5 kHz (Butterworth order 10, zero phase): the BS.1534-3 low anchor |

Two ratings per version, 0-100 sliders with the five MUSHRA labels (bad, poor, fair, good, excellent):
- Delivery of the marked spot, for the aspect of the item (pace, pause, pitch movement, steady volume, stress).
- Naturalness: "does it sound like a real, unedited recording? 0 = clearly processed, robotic or muffled; 100 =
  completely natural".

Each version must be played to the end once, and both sliders must be set, before the next screen. There is no
labelled (open) reference: listeners judge delivery by their own standard, and the hidden reference shows where a clean
reading sits on that scale. This deviates from BS.1534-3 (open reference, expert listeners).

Part 2 (two-choice pairs): for each item, original and fixed in random order (A, B), with one question in the direction
of the fix ("Which one is less rushed?", "... less slow and dragged out?", "... has the better pause?", "... has more
varied pitch (less flat)?", "... has steadier volume (less fading)?", "... stresses the marked word more clearly?").
Part 2 tests that the intended change is audible; Part 1 tests whether it is an improvement.

Other settings:
- Order: item order, A-D positions per item, pair order and A/B sides are shuffled per listener with a seed from the
  listener id (cyrb53 hash, mulberry32 generator). One practice item comes first and is not scored.
- Loudness: each stimulus is gained so that its context (the audio outside the finding's words) has the same BS.1770-4
  gated loudness (-23 LUFS); the finding's words keep their own level difference, which a volume fix changes on purpose.
  Peaks at or below -1 dBFS; 20 ms raised-cosine fades at both ends.
- Stimuli have random file names; the condition of each file is only in `key.json`, which listeners never get.
- Expected time: 12 to 15 minutes (12 items x about 55 s, 12 pairs x about 12 s, instructions).

## 3. Items (12 scored + 1 practice)

Each item is one finding that matches an injected edit of a known clean source; no 1 s context contains another injected
edit (`key.json` `other_edits_in_context` is empty for every item). Stimulus durations 2.3 to 4.9 s.

| item | dimension | clip (source) | finding | fix (own) | status, share of the wanted change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| s01 | pace | L3A (gallery, Reagan, level 3) | d1-003 RUSH | stretch x1.40 (slower) | capped, 0.54 |
| s02 | pace | L2A (gallery, Reagan, level 2) | d1-003 RUSH | stretch x1.40 | capped, 0.89 |
| s03 | pace | L2B (gallery, Reagan, level 2) | d1-001 DRAG | stretch x0.70 (faster) | capped, 0.99 |
| s04 | pause | L3A | d2-002 PAUSE_DROPPED | pause insert 0.71 s | applied, 1.00 |
| s05 | pause | L3B (gallery, Reagan, level 3) | d2-001 PAUSE_INTRUSIVE | pause shorten | applied, 1.00 |
| s06 | pause | greenman_edited (gallery, Greenman reading, R2) | d2-002 PAUSE_INTRUSIVE | pause shorten | applied, 1.00 |
| s07 | pitch | eisenhower_farewell_1961__single_L2A (final dataset, val split) | d3-002 MONOTONE | pitch excursion x1.5 | capped, 0.29 |
| s08 | pitch | obama_weekly_2009__G3 (final dataset, train split) | d3-002 MONOTONE | pitch excursion x1.5 | capped, 0.34 |
| s09 | volume | L2A | d5-002 FADE | gain +6.0 dB | capped, 0.77 |
| s10 | volume | L3A | d5-005 FADE | gain +6.0 dB | capped, 0.53 |
| s11 | volume | greenman_edited | d5-001 FADE | gain +5.1 dB (clipping limit) | capped, 0.46 |
| s12 | emphasis | L3B | d4-002 DEEMPHASIS | emphasis x1.40, 3 st, 4.3 dB | capped, 0.71 |
| practice | pause | L2B | d2-002 PAUSE_INTRUSIVE | pause shorten | applied, 1.00 (not scored) |

Why the pitch items come from the final dataset: with `pitch_method="own"` all three gallery MONOTONE findings (L3A,
G3, L2A d3-001) are skipped (the allowed edit changes the range by 0.0 to 0.9 st, below the 1.5 st floor), so the gallery
has no pitch item that the product changes. Of the 15 level-1/level-2 MONOTONE clips of the five non-Reagan train/val
baselines, 12 findings were flagged and 2 get an edit with "own" (s07, s08); the other 10 are skipped (0.4 to 1.3 st).
The test split was not read. The clean source of a dataset clip is its baseline recording.

## 4. Screening (decided before the data)

A listener is left out of all analyses when either holds:
1. The MUSHRA part is incomplete (fewer than 12 scored items with all 8 ratings).
2. On naturalness, the low anchor is rated at or above the hidden reference in more than 25 % of the scored items (more
   than 3 of 12). The screen uses only the reference and the anchor, never the original or the fixed version, so it
   cannot favour either side of H1 or H2.

With more than one session per listener id, the most complete session is used (then the latest finished one). Listeners
without headphones are kept; their number is reported.

## 5. Primary analysis

Unit: the per-listener mean over the 12 scored items of the paired difference fixed minus original (one value per
listener). Tests: exact Wilcoxon signed-rank (zeros dropped, average ranks for ties).

- H1 (delivery): delivery(fixed) > delivery(original). One-sided exact Wilcoxon on the listener means.
- H2 (naturalness, non-inferiority): naturalness(fixed) > naturalness(original) - 10. One-sided exact Wilcoxon on
  (listener mean + 10). Margin 10 points = half a MUSHRA category (20 points).
- Family-wise error: Holm-Bonferroni over H1 and H2, alpha 0.05.
- Effect sizes: grand mean difference with a 95 % interval from a two-way bootstrap (listeners and items resampled
  independently with replacement, 10 000 replicates, seed 20261010); for H2 also the one-sided 95 % lower bound.
- Generalisation over items: the same test on the 12 item means (each averaged over kept listeners); reported beside H1;
  it decides the wording (section 7).
- Minimum listeners: 6 kept listeners for any test (with 6, the smallest one-sided exact p is 1/64; one listener of the
  opposite sign at rank 1 still gives 2/64). Planned: owner + 8 to 10 friends. With fewer than 6, `score.py` reports
  means and intervals only, and no hypothesis is tested.
- Power (simulation of this exact test; normal listener means; dz = mean / SD of listener means; 2000 runs):
  6 listeners: dz 0.8 -> 0.49, 1.0 -> 0.65, 1.2 -> 0.78; 8 listeners: 0.59, 0.77, 0.87; 10 listeners: 0.69, 0.87, 0.95.
  Only large, consistent effects are detectable. A null result is not evidence of no effect.

## 6. Secondary analyses (descriptive; no claims of their own)

- Naturalness fixed - original, two-sided exact Wilcoxon (any naturalness cost).
- Manipulation check: delivery(reference) > delivery(original), one-sided. If it fails, the panel did not hear the
  injected flaws and H1 cannot be interpreted; report that.
- Naturalness reference - fixed; anchor check (naturalness reference - anchor).
- Share of the delivery gap closed: mean(fixed - original) / mean(reference - original), two-way bootstrap interval.
- Pairs: share of choices for the fixed version, overall and per dimension; overall one-sided exact Wilcoxon on
  (listener share - 0.5).
- Per dimension (pace 3, pause 3, pitch 2, volume 3, emphasis 1 items) and per item: the same differences with
  intervals. Too few items for tests; no per-dimension claim.
- Mixed-model sensitivity (only when statsmodels is installed): the fixed-minus-original difference per listener and
  item = intercept + crossed random intercepts of listener and item (REML), i.e. random slopes of the condition.
  Reported with its convergence flag; it does not replace the primary test.
- Owner sensitivity: the owner knows the design. Rerun with `--exclude <owner id>`; report both runs.

## 7. Wording of the result (decided before the data)

- H1 supported by listeners and by items: "In a blind listening test with N listeners, the fixed version was rated
  better on delivery than the flawed version (mean +X of 100 points, 95 % CI [a, b])."
- H1 supported by listeners only: the same, plus "the improvement was not consistent across the 12 spots".
- H1 not supported: "Listeners did not rate the fixed version better on delivery (mean X, 95 % CI [a, b])."
- H2 supported: add "and not more than 10 points less natural". H2 not supported: report the naturalness difference with
  its interval as a cost.
- Always state: synthetic flaws on 5 source recordings, 12 spots, untrained listeners who know the owner, pitch tested
  on 2 spots, per-dimension results descriptive.

## 8. Known limits

- All flaws are synthetic recipe edits of clean recordings; no naturally produced flaw is tested. Human listeners remove
  the closed loop for perception, not the synthetic-flaw limit.
- 12 items (pace 3, pause 3, pitch 2, volume 3, emphasis 1); no dimension alone supports a claim.
- 10 of 12 fixes are capped (share 0.29 to 0.99 of the wanted change); the test measures the shipped, capped fix.
- Durations differ between versions of the pace and pause items, so a listener can group versions by length.
- The page lists, per item, which two files form the original/fixed pair (Part 2 needs it); a listener who reads the
  page source learns the pair, not which file is fixed.
- No open reference and untrained listeners: MUSHRA-like, not a BS.1534-3 test.
- The low anchor is a bandwidth anchor; it bounds naturalness, not delivery.

## 9. Files

- `share/` (the only folder listeners get): `listening_test.html` (the whole test; ratings stay in the browser until the
  CSV download) and `stimuli/*.wav`.
- `key.json`: condition and provenance of every stimulus. Never share it.
- `make_kit.py`: rebuilds `share/` and `key.json` (a rebuild with changed audio changes `kit_version`).
- `score.py`: this analysis; writes `results.json` and `results.md`.
- `check_page.cjs`: headless end-to-end check (7 fake sessions, then `score.py`, then a comparison with the entered
  values); outputs in `check/`, which is not listener data.
- `sources/`: analysis results of the two dataset clips.
- `old/`: the earlier A/B kit (never run; superseded).
