## 2. Detector-free instruments (round 1, own and contour)

Definitions. Pause: Praat 'To TextGrid (silences)' (100 Hz, -25 dB re the loudest frame, silent and sounding intervals at least 0.05 s); measure = silent time from the middle of the word before the boundary to the middle of the word after it; agree when |measured change - intended change| <= 0.05 s. Timing: phonation time = region minus Praat silences; agree when |ln(after/before) - ln(planned ratio)| <= 0.05 (a quarter of the R1 D1 threshold). Syllable-nucleus rate (de Jong & Wempe 2009): intensity peaks (50 Hz floor) above the 99 % quantile - 25 dB, a dip > 2 dB to the next peak, voiced (Praat ac 30-450 Hz); rate = nuclei / phonation time; agree when |rate after - rate before / planned| <= 1 / phonation time after (one nucleus). Pitch: WORLD Harvest, the median five-word-window P90-P10 range (as the pipeline windows it). Level: A-weighted energy. Regions are placed with the stored word times and carried through the edit's time map; no measure uses the aligner's output on the edited audio, except the level span check.

## 3. Inverse recovery on the R1 clips (clean source known)

Definition: the gallery R1 clips are recipe edits of a clean parent (labels of build/work/kaggle_runs/last_run/x/data/dataset, which built the gallery; parent PCM sha256 and clip length checked). For a region, m_clean, m_flawed and m_fixed are the detector-free measures of section 2 on the parent (label parent word times), the gallery clip (label clip word times) and the repaired clip (clip times through the repair's time map). Recovered share = (m_fixed - m_flawed) / (m_clean - m_flawed): 1 = back at the clean value, 0 = unchanged, < 0 = further away, > 1 = past the clean value. Computed only when |m_clean - m_flawed| >= the resolution (ln phonation 0.05, silence 0.05 s, pitch 1 st, level 1.5 dB, nucleus rate one nucleus); else the row is 'no measurable injected change'. Measures: time = ln phonation time (primary) and ln nucleus rate; pause = silent time; pitch = Harvest P90-P10 of the region (>= 50 voiced frames); level = A-weighted dB; emphasis = the time, P90 and median Harvest, and level measures of the word.

## What the evidence supports and what it does not

- Supported: the edits execute as planned when measured without the detector (pause, phonation time, Harvest range, level frame check, with the misses listed above); on R1 clips the fixed regions move toward the clean source by the shares in section 3; processing-only PSOLA makes no flag change; sham edits of the same size on clean regions make flags mainly in the target dimension.
- Not supported: improved or more natural delivery (no listening test), validity on natural flaws (all flagged clips are recipe edits), the 'removed' count as 'fixed' (section 4: many removed findings keep part of the departure), the syllable-nucleus rate as a stable timing measure under strong compression.
