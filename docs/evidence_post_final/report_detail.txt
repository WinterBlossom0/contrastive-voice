10.1 Fix your speech: edit the reading, then analyse it again
-------------------------------------------------------------

Method (`repair.py`). For one finding or all, the participant's own recording is edited toward the finding's target and analysed again with the settings of the original result: WSOLA time scaling of the finding's words, pauses kept (pace, speech tempo); room tone inserted in the aligned gap, or the silent run shortened (pauses); PSOLA scaling of the speaker's own semitone excursions around the region median (pitch range), or as an option the reference melody in semitones, re-centred on the speaker's median [128, 129]; pitch raise, lengthening and gain together (emphasis); raised-cosine gain ramps that stop before clipping (volume). A time map carries each region to the edited audio; a per-word ledger keeps later rounds within the caps. Clarity, endings, whole-clip word-time unevenness and disfluency or low-effort patterns are not edited, with the reason given. This is the interventional counterpart [116] of the arithmetic "score with this penalty removed" (section 5). Caps are chosen limits extrapolated from whole-utterance listening tests (no test of local word-level edits was found): duration ×0.7 to ×1.4 per round, ×1.5 per word in all (Praat PSOLA loses 0.2–0.4 MOS at ×1.5 or ±3 st and 1.1–1.4 at ×2 [120]; slowing costs more than speeding [121, 122]; unvoiced speech is never slowed by ×2, which gives tonal noise [123]); pitch excursions ×1.5, frame change 3 st per round, 4 st in all (identity holds up to 3 st [124]), and a pitch edit that changes the range by less than 1.5 st is skipped [40]; gain 6 dB per word, without a quality source (the 1.5 dB JND [66] is a lower limit; gain does not restore effort [69]); inserted pause 2.0 s; emphasis ×1.4, 3 st, 6 dB. Wording. The page never says "Fixed": a finding is *No longer flagged* (with the applied share when capped), *Still flagged · partly changed*, *Not edited* or *Can't be fixed by editing*; yours, edited and target loop; the score change shows only if the unedited recording reproduces the original result.

Limits. (1) Closed loop: the edit targets the detector's measure and the same detector judges it, so *no longer flagged* shows detector consistency and edit execution, not better delivery; a published re-score of corrected contours [130] had the same limit. (2) All clips are synthetic edits, repaired by inverse operators of the same tool set; no natural flaw was tested. (3) No listening result: a blind MUSHRA-style test (12 spots, hidden clean reference, low anchor, delivery and naturalness, analysis fixed before data) is designed and pending; there is no perceptual evidence yet. (4) Own pitch rarely edits a flattened contour: all three gallery MONOTONE findings are skipped (allowed change 0.0–0.9 st), and 2 of 12 flagged MONOTONE findings of five other train and val speakers are edited; the reference-melody option replaces the speaker's melody and has no perceptual test. (5) Gain does not restore effort; an inserted pause has no boundary lengthening. (6) Without a reference only pause edits were checked: on 985 fit-phase readers, 32 longer-pause findings, all at unpunctuated boundaries where a median 8.4 % of other readers pause; 2 remove a majority phrase break. Novelty. Own-voice prosody correction [125, 131] and automatic scoring of modified learner audio [132, 133] exist. As far as the search found, what is added is a per-flag check that runs the same detector again on the edited recording, with processing-only, sham, dose and detector-free controls, and edit targets from the speaker's own typical values when there is no reference.

10.2 No reference: a rough check
--------------------------------

Design (`selfref.py`, `models/detector_selfref.json`). Each measure is compared with the speaker's own typical value: D1 and D9 window time against the time expected from CMUdict phone counts; D2 against the speaker's median pause of the boundary class (sentence end, other punctuation, none [141]); D3 against the median less a sentence-quarter declination offset [70, 97]; D5, D6 against the median. D4, D7, D8 need a reference and are absent; no whole-clip flags. Scales come from the train identity and placebo nulls; one h per key maximises the R1 objective on train under a budget of 0.5 false regions per minute over the train nulls plus 167 Gettysburg and poetry fit-phase readers (176.7 min): 95 regions in 190.0 min, 0.50/min pooled in-sample, 0.54/min on those readers alone. Accent Archive and EARS readers chose between variants, so they are validation readers: 0.59/min [0.48, 0.72] and 0.35/min [0.25, 0.48] (exact Poisson, readers not clustered). Validation core-8 decided 15 or more design choices, so its F1 (0.429) is optimistic. Word-duration norms from LibriTTS (Zen et al. 2019, CC BY 4.0; 79 speakers, 1053 readings; held-out D1 window spread 0.1467 with phone counts, 0.1317 with the norms) were tried for D1 (`norms_d1`); the rule set before that run (validation F1 above 0.429, validation nulls ≤ 0.5/min, each validation-reader rate ≤ the phone-count rate + 0.1/min) rejected them: F1 0.500 passed, Accent Archive 0.695 > 0.687/min failed. Phone counts are shipped. Text-expected durations follow [138–140]; such pause and monotone checks exist commercially [146]; no novelty is claimed.

Single test run (2026-10-10 09:45 IST; `detector_selfref.json` sha256 `6dd3adc5…` and `selfref.py` `025fd98a…`, equal to the repository files). Core 8, levels 2–3, one detection per change; F1 intervals are count-level bootstrap, not clustered by the 2 test speakers:

Level 1: 0 of 16; natural rushing 0 of 4. Unedited test copies: 12 false regions in 5.89 min (2.04/min; D1− 6, D2+ 6; lower bound 4.08/min), but each test baseline is six near-identical copies, so these are 2 distinct events: exact 95 % interval 0.25–7.4/min, inconclusive against the 0.5/min budget. `results.json` keeps no per-change outcomes, so no paired comparison is possible. The score gate fails: G5 clips score 97.85–100 on train and 97.90, 99.05 on val (gate ≤ 50); a self-relative measure moves with a flaw that covers the whole reading. The page therefore counts flagged spots instead of a score, calls the mode a rough check, and heads its edits *Hear it closer to your typical delivery* with a caution. The listed, unscored articulation rate uses a published norm [18] that 15–47 % of clean fit-phase readers fall outside. What a reference adds: with the same measures, test F1 is 0.36 without a reference and 0.812 / 0.879 with one.
